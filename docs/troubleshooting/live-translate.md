# Fix Live Translate problems

Live Translate requires Mach 1.1.0 or later, macOS 14.2 or later, an internet
connection, an active Mach account, and System Audio Recording permission.

## No audio source is available

1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
2. Confirm that Mach has System Audio Recording access.
3. Quit and reopen Mach after changing the permission.
4. Open Live Translate and select the call, browser, media app, or available Mac
   audio source you want to understand.

## No translation appears

1. Confirm that the selected source is playing audible content.
2. Confirm that the source and target languages are different.
3. Check your internet connection.
4. Stop and start one short test session.

Live Translate captures the selected Mac audio source. It does not use your
microphone for the translation stream.

## The session cannot connect or finish

1. Stop the current session.
2. Open Mach and choose **Reset Connection**.
3. Start a short test session.
4. Quit and reopen Mach only if the problem continues.

## Live Translate limit reached

Mach keeps an explanation visible when your current allowance has been reached.
Use **Request more time** in the Live Translate window through your signed-in
account. Retry after your allowance resets or additional access is approved.

Review [Mach pricing](https://usemach.app/pricing) and the
[Fair Use Policy](https://usemach.app/terms#fair-use) for current limits.

## Still stuck

Contact [Mach Support](https://usemach.app/support) with your macOS and Mach
versions, selected audio source, language pair, and approximate failure time.
Do not include private conversation content.

# Use Mach Live Translate

Live Translate turns audio already playing on your Mac into fast, readable
translation. It works beside the call, video, stream, lecture, or other audio
source instead of joining as a meeting bot.

## Before you begin

You need:

- Mach 1.1.0 or later;
- macOS 14 or later;
- an internet connection;
- an active Mach account; and
- macOS System Audio Recording permission.

Open Mach and choose **Live Translate**. Follow the macOS prompt if system-audio
permission has not been granted yet.

## Start translating

1. Open the call, video, radio, lecture, or stream you want to understand.
2. Open **Live Translate** from Mach.
3. Choose automatic detection or a source language.
4. Choose the language you want to read.
5. Select the available Mac audio source you want Mach to capture.
6. Choose **Start**.

Completed phrases appear as rolling cards. Each original phrase stays paired
with its translation so names, dates, and important wording remain easy to
check. Scroll back when you need to revisit an earlier part of the conversation.

## Control the view

- Turn **Original** off when you only want to read the translation.
- Change the source or target language when the conversation changes.
- Use **Stop** to finish the current session.
- Use **Start** to begin a new session.
- Close or hide the Live Translate window when you no longer need it.

## Choose an audio source

Live Translate captures the Mac audio source shown in its controls. It does not
use your microphone for the translation stream. Confirm the displayed source
before starting when sensitive audio or multiple apps are open.

## Privacy

Audio is processed only while a Live Translate session is running. Translation
content is kept in the visible session so you can follow and review the current
conversation. Stop the session when you no longer need translation.

Do not use Live Translate to record or process a conversation without the
permission required in your location or organization. Review important legal,
medical, financial, or official details with the original source or institution.

The current, controlling data-handling terms are in the
[Mach Privacy Policy](https://usemach.app/privacy).

## Troubleshooting

### No audio source is available

Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and confirm that Mach has System
Audio Recording access. Quit and reopen Mach after changing the permission.

### No translation appears

Confirm that the selected source is playing audible content, your internet
connection works, and the source and target languages are different. Stop and
start a short test session.

### The session cannot connect or finish

Stop the session, open Mach, choose **Reset Connection**, and try again. If the
problem continues, quit and reopen Mach.

For more help, visit [Mach Support](https://usemach.app/support) and include your
macOS version, Mach version, selected audio source, language pair, and the
approximate time of the failure. Do not include private conversation content.

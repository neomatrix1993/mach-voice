# Mach support

Choose the channel that matches the problem so it reaches the right place.

## Product help

Use the [Mach Help Center](https://usemach.app/help) for installation, output
modes, languages, accuracy, account, and troubleshooting guidance.

For help from a person, visit [Mach Support](https://usemach.app/support). Include:

- your macOS and Mach versions;
- the app or audio source you were using;
- what you expected and what happened;
- whether restarting Mach or choosing **Reset Connection** changed the result;
  and
- the approximate time of a connection problem, if relevant.

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, private dictated text, customer
data, or other sensitive content.

## Documentation problems

GitHub issues are appropriate for public documentation problems, such as a
broken link, unclear step, outdated screenshot, or missing public guide. Include
the affected page and a suggested correction when possible.

Do not use a public issue for account, billing, privacy, or confidential product
support.

## Security reports

Never report a suspected vulnerability in a public issue. Follow the private
reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Privacy and account requests

For account deletion, privacy questions, or data requests, follow the
[Mach Privacy Policy](https://usemach.app/privacy) and contact
[support@usemach.app](mailto:support@usemach.app) from the email address associated
with your Mach account.

# Security Policy

Mach takes the security and privacy of its users seriously. We appreciate
responsible reports that help us keep the Mach macOS app and services safe.

## Report a vulnerability

Do not disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public GitHub issue, discussion,
pull request, or other public channel.

Email [hello@usemach.app](mailto:hello@usemach.app) with the subject
`Security report`. Include, where possible:

- the affected Mach feature, app version, URL, or service;
- a clear description of the issue and its potential impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- relevant logs, screenshots, or test data with secrets and personal data removed;
- whether you have disclosed the issue to anyone else; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Please do not include passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, private user
content, or unnecessary personal data in your report.

## Responsible research

When investigating a suspected issue:

- test only accounts, devices, and data you own or are authorized to use;
- avoid privacy violations, service disruption, data destruction, and degraded
  availability;
- do not use social engineering, phishing, physical attacks, or denial of service;
- stop testing and report the issue if you encounter another person's data; and
- allow Mach a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address the issue
  before any public disclosure.

We will acknowledge reports as soon as practical, assess their impact, and keep
the reporter informed when there is meaningful progress. Resolution time varies
with severity and complexity. Submitting a report does not create a right to
compensation.

## Scope

This policy covers the Mach macOS app and services operated under
[`usemach.app`](https://usemach.app). Third-party services and unrelated products
are outside its scope; report issues in those systems to their respective owners.

For non-security problems, visit [Mach Support](https://usemach.app/support).
For information about data handling, read the [Privacy Policy](https://usemach.app/privacy)
and this repository's [privacy overview](docs/privacy.md).
